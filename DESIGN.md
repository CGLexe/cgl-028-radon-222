## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined intersection of brutalist precision, raw architectural structure, and artisanal warmth. Designed for a hybrid specialty coffee bar and productive workspace in the heart of Centro, Oaxaca, the system captures both the tactile calm of focused morning productivity and the kinetic surge of midday espresso. 

The emotional tone balances grounding tranquility with deliberate energy. The visual language eschews decorative froth in favor of structural clarity, confident typographic weight, and intentional negative space. Interfaces are disciplined, tactile, and modern—mirroring exposed concrete, polished blackened steel, warm local plaster, and high-voltage directional signage.

Core tenets of the style:
- **Architectural Framing:** Explicit boundaries, sharp functional grids, hairline delineation, and planar layering.
- **Controlled Kinetic Energy:** Strategic flashes of high-contrast yellow to provoke momentum, direct attention, and symbolize energy.
- **Editorial Utility:** Monolithic geometric headers matched with quiet, hyper-legible body typography designed for long reading and rapid scanning.

## Colors

The palette derives from the physical sensory environment: cast shadow, light-washed stucco, dark roasted beans, and radiant industrial signage.

- **Primary (`#121212`):** Deep Matte Charcoal/Black. Used for primary typography, structural linework, high-contrast buttons, and heavy framing.
- **Secondary (`#F5BF03`):** Radiant Energetic Yellow. Inspired by physical neon accents and brand markers ("Tu dosis diaria de energía"). Applied strictly as an energetic catalyst: status beacons, primary action highlights, active selection indicators, and key metric badges.
- **Tertiary (`#E5B000`):** Burnished Ochre Yellow. Provides tactile depth, hover states for energetic triggers, and accessible warm contrast against light surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#F5F2EB` / Canvas `#FDFBF7`):** Warm Architectural Bone & Plaster. Replaces sterile digital white with natural earthen brightness, grounding the UI in organic warmth while maintaining crisp contrast.

Dark mode flips the visual hierarchy into evening studio mode: rich mineral blacks (`#121212`, `#1C1B1B`) dominate the foundation, while typography shifts to soft parchment (`#F5F2EB`), keeping the vibrant yellow (`#F5BF03`) radiant.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a dual-engine architecture:
- **Headline & Label Axis (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, brutalist-industrial edge. The glyphs introduce an engineered rhythm suited for room bookings, pricing tiers, desk passes, and menu items. Labels utilize uppercase styling with subtle tracking to echo architectural blueprint callouts and workshop typography.
- **Reading & Editorial Axis (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Softens the raw construction with humanist geometry, open apertures, and balanced x-height. It ensures effortless comprehension in long menu descriptions, membership terms, and reservation management.

Keep line heights tight for oversized display headers to preserve density, and ensure generous leading on body text to support leisurely reading in bright cafe lighting.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes a disciplined 12-column grid system anchored by modular 4px base increments. Negative space is treated as a solid structural element rather than void.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 48px outer canvas margin, 24px column gutter. Layout blocks feature expansive lateral rhythm, allowing cafe photography, availability schedules, and coffee bean origins to breathe.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px outer canvas margins and 20px column gutter. Side panels and reservation sidebars collapse into sticky docked trays.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout, 20px outer margin, 16px gutter. Complex booking tables and day-pass selectors transition into vertical segmented stacks.

Layouts align to crisp horizontal baseline guides. Avoid center-aligned long-form text; favor left-aligned architectural blocks anchored against hairline borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects diffused, blurry skeuomorphic drop shadows in favor of **structural delineation and architectural planar elevation**.

1. **Hairline Outlines (Level 0 - Default Flat):** Cards, modular tiles, and sections reside directly on the bone canvas (`#FDFBF7`), separated exclusively by 1px solid hairline borders in `#121212` at 12% opacity (or 100% solid `#121212` for primary feature panels).
2. **Hard Cast Shadows (Level 1 - Interactive Elevation):** Floating components, hovered cards, and active modals receive a distinct architectural offset shadow: `2px 2px 0px 0px #121212`. For primary callout cards or energy highlights, the offset shadow takes on the accent color: `3px 3px 0px 0px #F5BF03`.
3. **Overlays & Modals (Level 2):** Modals utilize a backdrop scrim of `#121212` at 65% opacity without blur, preserving structural sharpness across the application.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **soft-industrial geometry** (Level 1). 

- Standard interactive controls, form elements, and basic cards adhere strictly to `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii. 
- Large structural surfaces and modals use `0.5rem` (8px). 
- Pills and circular buttons are avoided for general actions, reserved solely for live desk-occupancy status markers (`active`, `reserved`, `focus mode`).

This restrained radius honors the physical corners of millwork, stone counters, and metal fabrication found in the cafe space while mitigating the visual harshness of absolute 90-degree digital corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#121212`, text `#FDFBF7`, border 1px solid `#121212`, 4px border radius. Hover state translates `-1px, -1px` with a persistent `#F5BF03` 2px offset box-shadow.
- **Energy / Accent:** Background `#F5BF03`, text `#121212`, 1px solid `#121212`, font weight bold. Used for key conversion moments (e.g., "Reservar Espacio", "Ordenar Café"). Hover transitions to tertiary `#E5B000`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#121212` (25% opacity), text `#121212`. Hover triggers 100% border opacity and `#F5F2EB` fill.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FDFBF7` with 1px border `#121212` (20% opacity). Typography in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to solid 1.5px `#121212`, accompanied by a subtle `#F5BF03` focus ring (`0 0 0 1px #F5BF03`).
- Labels: Set in `Space Grotesk` uppercase (`label-sm`), fixed above inputs with explicit tracking.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- Constructed with `#FDFBF7` surface and bounded by 1px solid border (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.12)`).
- Card headers feature an architectural divider line separating metadata (e.g., room capacity, power outlet specs, single-origin details) from descriptive text.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Category Chips:** Rectangular (4px radius), 1px border, filled with `#F5F2EB`. Text set in `Space Grotesk` 11px uppercase.
- **Live Status Badges:** Displaying cowork capacity or booth availability. Features a live pulsating 6px circular dot (`#F5BF03` for active/occupied, `#121212` for closed, `#34C759` for available space).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom square check-boxes with 2px corner radius; unselected has a 1.5px `#121212` border.
- Selected state fills with `#121212` housing a high-contrast `#F5BF03` checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Espresso & Roast Origin Badge:** Minimalist grid module highlighting altitude, variety, process, and tasting notes separated by fine hairline dividers.
- **Workspace Desk Matrix:** Visual floor plan selector adopting monochrome cubicle framing with vivid yellow highlighting for currently reserved slots.
